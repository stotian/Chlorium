# R4-19 - Native command-stamped Area producer shadow

**Keep the oracle and shadow schedule. Native phase elimination remains blocked.**

The next architectural step required the real producer contract, rather than
another transport variant or a replacement-only arithmetic model. The fixture
executes complete, fingerprinted `moveAreaObject`, `resetAreaObjectValues` and
`updateAreaObjectLastValues` bodies extracted from the owner's GD 2.2081.
Direct calls and the one RIP-relative constant are relocated into a private RX
page. The grow-vector target terminates; fixtures provision tracking capacity.
No game instructions or game files are distributed.

Two moves of +2 and +4 at the same command stamp produce +6, an accumulated
float offset of 6, one tracking registration and two section callbacks.
R4-18's reset/apply replacement program produces +4 in its own domain. Its
large closed-model speedups therefore cannot justify folding these GD calls.
The Windows move path also dirties object fields and calls the section hook
for a blocked horizontal move; a zero effective displacement is not sufficient
to declare the native operation inert.

The immutable shadow retains the native schedule. It compares all object bytes,
layer writes, tracking identities/order, and every fixture callback's complete
object/layer/tracking snapshot. An observer may read other objects and write
another object's position. Signed stamps can decrease; append and reused-slot
tracking paths are covered. Input and tracking budgets reject unsupported work
before shadow publication. Results and counts are in `evidence.json`.

Three broken variants are rejected: last-writer folding, object-major replay,
and transient tracking corruption hidden from final-state checks. The last
negative control addresses a gap identified by the independent inspector.
Four complete bodies are inventoried, including the entire linear byte range
of `transformAreaObjects`; direct jumps must land on decoded instructions.
This is not a resolution of all Area readers or indirect callees.

## Limits and next step

This is a native isolated test, not a GD run, benchmark or FPS gain. No runtime
target includes this code and no native work is suppressed. The real section
handler, collision system, reset=false callbacks, allocator growth, checkpoints,
getUnmodifiedPosition, Area falloff/easing and external hooks remain unproved.
The bounded finite domain includes signed zeros/subnormals and 16 SSE controls;
it does not certify NaN/Inf, unmasked traps, x87 or arbitrary FP-control changes.

Next: shadow the producer of the actual per-object translation operand and its
helper-transform reads. A group program must preserve these publication points
or establish a narrower closed span before removing repeated traversal/math.
